GDTP (Y14.5) - Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing

Are you ready to achieve the gold standard in engineering documentation and unlock your career potential? This comprehensive course is your definitive guide to mastering Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing (GD&T) according to the rigorous ASME Y14.5-2018 standard. Designed specifically as an intensive preparation program for the Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing Professional (GDTP) certification exams (both Technologist and Senior levels), this course will transform your understanding of technical drawings from basic interpretation to expert-level application.

This course is not just a theoretical overview; it is a deep, practical dive into the language of engineering. We will demystify the dense Y14.5 standard and provide you with the critical thinking skills needed to apply its principles to real-world design, manufacturing, and inspection challenges.

What you will master in this course:

  • Core Fundamentals: Go beyond basic definitions to truly understand the Feature Control Frame, all 14 geometric symbols, material condition modifiers (MMC, LMC, RFS), and the foundational rules, including the Envelope Principle (Rule #1).

  • Datum Systems That Work: Learn to select and apply functional datum reference frames (DRFs) using the 3-2-1 rule. Master complex concepts like datum shift, Maximum Material Boundary (MMB), and the practical application of datum targets on irregular or flexible parts.

  • Geometric Controls in Depth: Gain expert-level command of all geometric controls, with a special focus on the most powerful and common ones: Position and Profile. We will cover critical distinctions like Circular vs. Total Runout and the modern replacements for the obsolete concentricity and symmetry symbols.

  • Advanced Applications & Analysis: Tackle the most challenging GD&T topics, including composite position and profile tolerancing, calculating virtual and resultant conditions, and performing both worst-case and statistical tolerance stack-up analyses.

  • The Future of GD&T: Digital & MBD: Stay ahead of the curve by understanding how GD&T is implemented in a Model-Based Definition (MBD) environment per the ASME Y14.41 standard. Learn about semantic PMI and its role in the digital thread, AI-driven analysis, and tolerancing for additive manufacturing.

This course is built around a powerful, exclusive question bank of over 300 GDTP-style multiple-choice questions, each with detailed, per-option explanations and overall summaries that reference the specific clauses in the standard. By the end of this course, you will not only be prepared to pass the GDTP exam but will also be a more effective and valuable engineering professional.

Enroll today and take the definitive step toward becoming a certified GD&T expert.
