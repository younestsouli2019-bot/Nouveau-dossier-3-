Confined Space Entry Supervisor

BackBundles

Are you ready to take command of one of the most high-stakes jobs in industrial and construction safety? The Confined Space Entry Supervisor role is not just a title; it's a critical responsibility that protects lives. This comprehensive course is your direct path to mastering the OSHA 29 CFR 1910.146 and 1926 standards, giving you the knowledge, confidence, and qualifications to lead your team safely and pass your certification exam.

This isn't a dry, theoretical lecture. We dive deep into the real-world scenarios you will face on the job. From interpreting complex atmospheric monitor readings to making the tough call on a rescue plan, this course is designed to build true competence. We demystify the dense legal language of OSHA regulations and turn it into practical, actionable knowledge you can apply from day one.

Throughout this course, you will master:

  • The Four Critical Roles: Understand the distinct, non-overlapping duties of the Entry Supervisor, Attendant, Authorized Entrant, and the supporting Rescue Team.

  • Permit Systems That Work: Learn to develop, issue, oversee, and cancel a compliant confined space entry permit. We'll cover every required field and the "why" behind them.

  • Atmospheric Testing & Hazard Control: Move beyond just reading numbers. You'll learn the proper testing sequence (O₂, LEL, Toxics), what the results really mean, and how to implement crucial controls like Lockout/Tagout (LOTO), ventilation, and positive isolation (blinding/blanking).

  • Real-World Scenarios: We'll analyze complex situations involving combustible dust, chemical hazards, inert atmospheres, and physical hazards like engulfment and entrapment.

  • Rescue & Emergency Planning: Learn to distinguish between non-entry and entry rescue, evaluate the capabilities of an on-site or off-site rescue service, and understand the supervisor's critical role during an emergency.

  • OSHA Compliance: Gain a deep understanding of the specific requirements of 29 CFR 1910.146 (General Industry) and 1926 Subpart AA (Construction), ensuring your operations are fully compliant.

This course is specifically designed for:

  • Aspiring or newly appointed Foremen, Supervisors, and Managers in construction and industrial settings.

  • Experienced Safety Professionals (EHS, OHS) seeking certification or a comprehensive refresher.

  • Maintenance Planners and Team Leaders who oversee work in permit spaces.

  • Anyone preparing for a Confined Space Entry Supervisor certification exam.

With engaging video lessons, downloadable guides, and a massive bank of certification-style practice questions, you will have everything you need. Enroll today and gain the confidence and competence to lead your team safely and achieve your certification.

What is primarily taught in your course?

  • How to distinguish between confined spaces and permit-required confined spaces (PRCS) according to OSHA 29 CFR 1910.146.

  • The specific duties, responsibilities, and limitations of the Entry Supervisor, Attendant, and Authorized Entrant.

  • The complete lifecycle of a confined space entry permit, from development and issuance to termination and cancellation.

  • How to interpret atmospheric test results (O₂, LEL, CO, H₂S) and implement appropriate hazard controls like ventilation and Lockout/Tagout (LOTO).

  • How to evaluate rescue service capabilities and coordinate non-entry and entry rescue procedures.

  • How to confidently prepare for certification exams through extensive practice with scenario-based questions aligned with OSHA standards.
