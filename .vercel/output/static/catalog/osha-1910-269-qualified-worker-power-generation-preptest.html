Are you preparing for your OSHA 1910.269 Qualified Worker examination? Do you want to ensure your knowledge of high-voltage safety procedures is sharp, accurate, and up-to-date? The safety of you and your crew depends on it.

This comprehensive practice exam is your essential tool for validating your skills and identifying areas for improvement before taking your official qualification test. Designed specifically for electrical workers in the power generation, transmission, and distribution industry, this course contains hundreds of realistic, scenario-based questions that go beyond simple memorization and test your ability to apply critical safety standards in the field.

Our questions are meticulously crafted to mirror the format and difficulty of the real exam, covering all critical domains of the OSHA 1910.269 standard, including:

  • General Safety, Preparation, and Training: Job briefings, hazard analysis, and qualification requirements.

  • Live-Line Work & Minimum Approach Distances (MAD): Rubber gloving, hot stick techniques, barehanding, and calculating proper clearances for various voltage levels.

  • De-energizing and Lockout/Tagout (LOTO): Simple, complex, and group lockout procedures to ensure positive control of hazardous energy.

  • Personal Protective Grounding: Principles of Equipotential Zone (EPZ) grounding, proper application/removal, and fault current considerations.

  • Personal Protective Equipment (PPE): Selection, inspection, and use of rubber goods, arc-rated clothing, and other protective equipment.

  • Tools and Equipment: Inspection and safe use of live-line tools, insulated aerial lifts, and high-voltage test instruments.

  • Emergency Procedures: Responding to electrical contact incidents, fires, and performing pole-top/bucket rescues.

Each question comes with a detailed, referenced explanation for every answer option—not just the correct one. This allows you to understand the 'why' behind the rules, correcting common misconceptions and reinforcing critical safety principles. The mix of multiple-choice (single correct answer) and multi-select (multiple correct answers) questions will challenge your ability to apply the standard to the real-world situations you face on the job every day.

Don't leave your qualification to chance. Identify your knowledge gaps, build your confidence, and master the core concepts of OSHA 1910.269. Enroll now and take the next step toward ensuring a safe and successful career in the high-voltage utility industry.
