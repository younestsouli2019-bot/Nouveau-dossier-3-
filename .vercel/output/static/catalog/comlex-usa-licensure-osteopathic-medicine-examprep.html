Are you preparing for the COMLEX-USA exam and striving to become a licensed osteopathic physician (DO)? This course provides a rigorous and realistic exam preparation experience tailored for COMLEX Level 1, Level 2-CE, and Level 3 candidates.

With a focus on high-difficulty questions, this course ensures you master core osteopathic principles, clinical reasoning, and patient-centered care—exactly what the National Board of Osteopathic Medical Examiners (NBOME) evaluates.

What Makes This Course Unique?

  • Realistic COMLEX Question Format – Practice with multiple-choice (MCQs) and multi-select (MSQs) questions mirroring the actual exam.

  • Detailed Answer Explanations – Each answer includes a comprehensive rationale, ensuring deep understanding.

  • Case-Based Clinical Scenarios – Master patient diagnosis, OMM application, and osteopathic structural findings through high-yield patient vignettes.

  • High-Yield COMLEX Blueprint Coverage – Target Osteopathic Manipulative Treatment (OMT), Pathophysiology, Pharmacology, and Clinical Decision-Making.

  • Updated to Match NBOME Standards – Aligns with the latest COMLEX-USA exam structure and competency domains.

What You’ll Learn:

  • Foundational Biomedical Sciences – Anatomy, physiology, pathology, microbiology, and pharmacology.

  • Clinical Decision-Making – Master differential diagnosis, lab interpretations, and imaging analysis.

  • Osteopathic Principles & Manipulative Medicine (OPP & OMM) – Understand somatic dysfunctions, Chapman’s reflexes, viscerosomatic reflexes, and OMT techniques (HVLA, counterstrain, myofascial release, etc.).

  • Emergency & Trauma Care – Learn ACLS protocols, pain management, and trauma assessment.

  • Preventive Medicine & Ethics – Understand public health, epidemiology, and legal/ethical considerations in osteopathic practice.

Who This Course is For:

  • COMLEX-USA Level 1, Level 2-CE, and Level 3 candidates

  • Osteopathic medical students (OMS-2, OMS-3, OMS-4)

  • DO residents looking to reinforce OPP knowledge

  • Anyone seeking high-quality COMLEX practice exams with expert explanations
