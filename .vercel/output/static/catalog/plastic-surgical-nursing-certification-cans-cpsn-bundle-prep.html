Elevate your nursing career and achieve dual certification with this comprehensive prep course for both the Certified Aesthetic Nurse Specialist (CANS) and Certified Plastic Surgical Nurse (CPSN) exams. This bundle is expertly designed for registered nurses and nurse practitioners who are ready to validate their expertise and become recognized leaders in the dynamic fields of aesthetic and plastic surgical nursing.

Administered by the Plastic Surgical Nursing Certification Board (PSNCB), the CANS and CPSN certifications are the gold standard, demonstrating your commitment to patient safety and the highest standards of practice.[1][2] This course is your all-in-one resource to confidently master the core competencies required for both examinations.

What You Will Master:

  • Aesthetic Nursing (CANS Focus): Dive deep into the key domains of aesthetic practice. You will gain a thorough understanding of facial anatomy and physiology, the science and application of cosmetic injectables like neurotoxins and dermal fillers, and the physics and safety protocols for laser and light-based therapies.[3] We will also cover clinical skincare, chemical peels, and the critical ethical and legal considerations specific to the aesthetic field.[3]

  • Plastic Surgical Nursing (CPSN Focus): Broaden your expertise to the full spectrum of plastic and reconstructive surgery. This section covers in-depth patient assessment, preoperative and postoperative care, management of surgical procedures for the head, neck, breast, and body, and strategies for minimizing complications.[4][5] You will learn to apply the nursing process across both reconstructive and cosmetic surgical contexts.[6]
