Are you ready to become an Autodesk Certified Professional (ACP) in 2025? This comprehensive prep course is designed to equip you with the skills and knowledge you need to confidently pass the ACP certification exams for AutoCAD, Revit, Fusion 360, and Inventor. This is not just a theory review, but a hands-on, practice-driven approach to mastering the core concepts and advanced techniques you’ll encounter in the official ACP exams.

This course covers four key Autodesk software packages, and will provide a comprehensive review for the certification. First, we'll dive deep into AutoCAD, focusing on advanced 2D and 3D modeling, parametric design, and efficient drafting workflows. You'll learn the best practices for creating complex geometry, working with blocks and layers, and managing large drawings efficiently. Next, we will explore Revit, where you'll learn to build information models, create complex families, and use worksharing techniques effectively. We will focus on architectural and structural aspects of Revit. For Fusion 360, you will learn to master parametric modeling, direct modeling, surface and mesh editing and simulation workflows including FEA, generative design and CAM tools. Finally, for Inventor, you will learn best practices for assembly design, sheet metal creation, advanced constraint application and dynamic simulation, and the use of iLogic.

Throughout this course, you will gain access to a wide variety of practice questions and mock exams that simulate the real ACP certification environment. These practice materials cover all key topics and difficulty levels, ensuring you're well-prepared for the exam's challenges. Expert tips and strategies from certified instructors will provide valuable insights into the types of questions and the best approaches for solving them.

This course is ideal for experienced CAD users, designers, engineers, and students aiming for ACP certification. By completing this course, you will:

  • Master advanced techniques in AutoCAD, Revit, Fusion 360, and Inventor.

  • Become proficient in the workflows for designing, modelling and drafting.

  • Gain hands-on experience with practice questions and mock exams.

  • Learn valuable tips and strategies to pass the ACP certification exams with confidence.

  • Boost your career prospects by adding the Autodesk Certified Professional certification to your resume.

Enroll today and take the first step towards achieving your ACP certification!
