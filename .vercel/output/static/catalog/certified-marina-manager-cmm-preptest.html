Are you ready to take the final step in your marina management career and earn the prestigious Certified Marina Manager (CMM) designation? The CMM exam is a comprehensive test of your practical knowledge, critical thinking, and decision-making abilities. It's not about memorizing facts; it's about proving you can lead and manage a modern marina facility effectively and ethically. This course is the ultimate tool to ensure you are prepared for that challenge.

This comprehensive CMM practice exam course provides you with 300 unique, scenario-based multiple-choice and multi-select questions meticulously designed to mirror the real exam's format and difficulty. Each question is crafted to test your application of knowledge in realistic situations, focusing on common pitfalls and complex decision-making.

We go beyond just questions. Every single answer option comes with a detailed explanation, clarifying not only why the correct answer is right, but also why the distractors are wrong. This method deepens your understanding of the core concepts and helps you avoid common mistakes.

This course covers all five core domains of the CMM blueprint:

  • Marina Operations Management: Master everything from emergency planning and preventative maintenance to electrical safety and hurricane preparedness.

  • Business Management and Finance: Dive deep into budgeting, financial statement analysis (P&L, Balance Sheet), insurance, contracts, and capital planning.

  • Environmental Compliance and Stewardship: Confidently handle issues like spill response (SPCC), stormwater management (SWPPP), hazardous waste, and sustainability initiatives.

  • Marketing, Sales, and Customer Experience: Learn to build a strong brand, manage your reputation, handle difficult customers, and create effective marketing strategies.

  • Human Resources and Leadership: Tackle complex employee relations issues, including ADA, FLSA, harassment, and effective leadership strategies to build a strong team.

By the time you complete this course, you will have honed your critical thinking skills and gained the confidence to walk into the CMM exam prepared for any scenario they throw at you. Enroll today and take the definitive step toward becoming a Certified Marina Manager.
