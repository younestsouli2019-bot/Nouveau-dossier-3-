Are you ready to become a certified instructor and empower others with life-saving skills? This comprehensive course is designed to provide you with the knowledge, practical skills, and teaching expertise required to confidently pass the 2025 First Aid, CPR, and AED Instructor certification exam.

This course is not just another review! It goes far beyond the basics. You will master critical areas, including adult, child, and infant CPR techniques, advanced first aid skills, the effective use of automated external defibrillators (AEDs), and the very important legal and ethical considerations for instructors. The course meticulously covers all the crucial information you will need to know, along with the test prep strategies to ensure your success on the Instructor exam.

We will deep dive into effective teaching methods, adult learning principles, and curriculum development, equipping you with the skills to create engaging and impactful learning experiences. You'll learn how to manage classroom dynamics, provide constructive feedback, and adapt your instruction to diverse learning styles. This course also provides insight into common errors, effective ways to communicate and correct, and real-world scenarios, allowing you to hone your knowledge.

Upon completion of this course, you'll not only be thoroughly prepared for the instructor exam but also possess the skills and confidence to train and certify others. Take the first step toward becoming a knowledgeable and inspiring First Aid, CPR, and AED instructor today
