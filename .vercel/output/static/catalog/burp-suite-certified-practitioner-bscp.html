Burp Suite Certified Practitioner (BSCP)

BackBundles

Are you ready to prove your mastery of web penetration testing and earn one of the most respected practical certifications in the industry? The Burp Suite Certified Practitioner (BSCP) exam is notoriously challenging because it doesn't test theory—it tests your real-world ability to compromise modern web applications using Burp Suite Professional. This course is your comprehensive, hands-on guide to mastering the techniques required to pass.

We will move far beyond basic scanning and manual testing. This course is built around the official BSCP syllabus and focuses on developing a robust testing methodology. You will learn not just what the tools do, but how to chain them together to exploit complex, multi-stage vulnerabilities.

Throughout this course, you will dive deep into:

  • Advanced Discovery & Scanning: Learn to configure Burp's crawler and scanner for maximum coverage on modern SPAs, APIs, and complex applications. You'll master scan diagnostics, custom configurations, and how to effectively manage scope.

  • Manual Exploitation Mastery: This is the core of the course. We will manually exploit a wide range of vulnerabilities, including HTTP Request Smuggling, complex Blind XXE, Web Cache Poisoning, Race Conditions, and advanced SQL injection. You will learn how to use Repeater, Intruder, and Collaborator not just as tools, but as an extension of your own analytical abilities.

  • Automation and Efficiency: Learn to handle complex session management with macros and session handling rules. Automate sophisticated fuzzing attacks with custom Intruder configurations and learn when to use extensions like Turbo Intruder to find vulnerabilities that other testers miss.

  • Exam-Ready Methodology: Each section concludes with hands-on labs designed to mimic the style and complexity of the BSCP exam. You will learn a repeatable methodology for approaching black-box targets, identifying the weak points, and chaining low-impact findings into critical vulnerabilities.

This course is not for beginners. It is for dedicated professionals who understand the fundamentals of web security and want to bridge the gap between intermediate knowledge and expert-level, practical exploitation.

Enroll today and take the final step toward becoming a Burp Suite Certified Practitioner.
