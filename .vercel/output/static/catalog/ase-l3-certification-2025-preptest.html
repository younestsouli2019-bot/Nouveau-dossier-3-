  • Battery Management Systems (BMS): Master the intricacies of battery pack technology, cell balancing, thermal management, and performance metrics.

  • Regenerative Braking: Understand how EVs and HEVs recapture braking energy, boosting efficiency, and improving range.

  • Charging Systems: Learn about different charging levels and standards, and how charging circuits function.

  • Troubleshooting and Diagnostics: Develop practical skills in diagnosing common issues with high-voltage systems and control systems, utilizing CAN bus and diagnostic techniques.
