Are you preparing for the EASA Module 2 Aircraft Maintenance Technician exam and finding the physics concepts challenging? This course provides you with the knowledge and practical skills to confidently tackle the exam and confidently apply these concepts in your career as an aircraft maintenance technician. EASA Module 2 can be a demanding module, so this course has been designed to make the topics more understandable, and help you retain the information through worked examples and practice questions.

This comprehensive course will thoroughly cover all key areas of the EASA Module 2 Physics syllabus. You will gain a strong understanding of mechanics, including motion, forces, and energy. We will also explore thermodynamics and how it applies to aircraft engines and environmental systems. You will then progress to exploring waves, optics and the various types of light transmission, and finally explore electromagnetism, so that you have an understanding of the basis of aircraft electrical systems. This course is designed to equip you to deal with physics at all levels.

You'll learn through detailed video lectures that break down each concept into understandable parts, supported by worked examples and real-world applications. Additionally, numerous quizzes will help solidify your understanding of each area, and a 250 question practice exam will prepare you for the certification.

By the end of this course, you’ll not only be fully prepared to pass the EASA Module 2 Physics exam, but also possess a deep understanding of the physics principles essential for successful aircraft maintenance. This course is suitable for aspiring aircraft maintenance technicians, those preparing for the EASA Module 2 exam, or anyone who wishes to develop a solid foundation in the physics that underpins aircraft design, operation and maintenance. Get certified with confidence!
