Are you ready to become a Siemens Certified SCADA Engineer in 2025? This comprehensive preparation course is designed to equip you with the knowledge and skills needed to confidently pass the challenging certification exam. This course provides focused preparation for the Siemens Certified SCADA Engineer certification.

We'll dive deep into the core concepts, technologies, and best practices essential for success in the field of Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) systems. You'll gain a thorough understanding of:

  • Human-Machine Interfaces (HMIs): Master the design, configuration, and operation of HMIs, including user interface design, alarm management, and data visualization techniques. We will explore the various HMI software packages available to you, and you will become fully acquainted with setting up an effective HMI.

  • Remote Terminal Units (RTUs): Learn the architecture, functionality, and communication protocols of RTUs. Understand how RTUs collect data from the field, perform local control, and transmit data to the central control center.

  • Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs): Gain practical experience with PLCs, including ladder logic programming, I/O configuration, and the implementation of control strategies. Learn the PLC programming, and troubleshooting.

  • SCADA Network Security: With the increasing prevalence of cyber threats, securing SCADA networks is crucial. This course will cover the critical aspects of SCADA network security, including firewall configuration, intrusion detection systems, authentication mechanisms, and risk assessment.

  • SCADA Protocols: We will have an extensive look at the major protocols.

Throughout the course, you'll have access to practice quizzes and simulations and you will be able to test your skills in various scenarios.

Whether you're an experienced automation professional or new to the SCADA field, this course provides the practical knowledge and exam preparation materials you need to excel. Enroll now and take the first step towards becoming a Siemens Certified SCADA Engineer!
