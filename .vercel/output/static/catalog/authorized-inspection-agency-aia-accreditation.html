Are you ready to advance your career in the pressure equipment industry? This comprehensive course provides an in-depth mastery of the requirements for becoming a world-class Authorized Inspection Agency (AIA) and is essential for any professional seeking to become a Commissioned Authorized Inspector (AI) or Supervisor (AIS).

Navigating the complex world of ASME and National Board accreditation can be challenging. This program is specifically designed to demystify the process, giving you the knowledge and confidence to lead and execute inspection activities with the highest degree of professionalism and compliance. We will dive deep into the foundational document, ASME CA-1, "Conformity Assessment Requirements," to ensure you master the principles of independence, quality management, and ethical conduct that are the bedrock of an AIA's operations.

Throughout the course, we will explore the critical relationship between the AIA and the Jurisdictional Authorities, providing you with strategies for negotiating agreements and harmonizing the requirements of the ASME/NBIC codes with local laws and regulations. You will learn to manage complex compliance scenarios, from withholding certification on non-conforming items to reporting incidents and handling disputes.

This curriculum is packed with real-world scenarios, case studies, and practical insights that go beyond simple theory. We will cover the specific inspection protocols for new construction (ASME) and in-service activities (NBIC), including repairs and alterations. By the end of this course, you will not just understand the rules; you will be prepared to implement a robust and compliant inspection program, making you an invaluable asset to any organization in the pressure equipment field. Enroll today to take the definitive step toward becoming an expert in Authorized Inspection.
