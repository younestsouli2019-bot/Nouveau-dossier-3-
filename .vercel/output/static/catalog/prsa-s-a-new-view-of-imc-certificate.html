Are your marketing efforts stuck in the past? Do you struggle with siloed teams, inconsistent messaging, and proving the true value of your campaigns? In today's crowded digital landscape, the old rules of marketing no longer apply. It's time for a "New View."

This comprehensive course is your definitive guide to mastering modern Integrated Marketing Communications (IMC), built on the official framework of the Public Relations Society of America (PRSA). We move beyond outdated models to give you a strategic, stakeholder-centric approach that drives measurable business results.

You will begin by deconstructing traditional marketing (the 4Ps) and rebuilding your foundation on the customer-focused SIVA model (Solution, Information, Value, Access). You'll learn not just what IMC is, but how to instill an integrated, ethical, and behavior-driven mindset within your organization.

Next, you will dive deep into the heart of modern strategy: the audience. You will learn to conduct behavioral segmentation, develop research-based customer personas, map omnichannel journeys, and use empathy to create messaging that resonates and solves real problems.

From there, we master the channels. You will learn to orchestrate campaigns using the powerful PESO model (Paid, Earned, Shared, Owned), ensuring every touchpoint works in synergy. We will cover everything from content adaptation and Web3 community activation to navigating the "dark social" funnel.

Finally, you will learn to prove your worth. This course teaches you to measure what matters, moving beyond vanity metrics to focus on ROI, customer lifetime value (CLV), attribution modeling, and brand lift. We'll equip you with agile implementation methods—using sprints and test-learn-adapt cycles—to make your team faster, smarter, and more responsive.

Whether you are preparing for the PRSA certificate exam or seeking to become a strategic leader in your organization, this course will provide you with the framework, skills, and confidence to execute world-class integrated campaigns. Enroll today to transform your approach to marketing.
