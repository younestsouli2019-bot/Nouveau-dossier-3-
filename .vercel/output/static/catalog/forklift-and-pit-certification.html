Are you ready to become a safe, confident, and federally compliant forklift operator? Or are you a safety manager tasked with ensuring your team meets the highest standards? This course is your definitive guide to mastering Powered Industrial Truck (PIT) operation according to OSHA's rigorous 29 CFR 1910.178 standard.

Operating a forklift is more than just driving; it’s a responsibility that demands skill, knowledge, and an unwavering commitment to safety. Accidents are preventable, and proper training is the single most effective tool to protect yourself, your coworkers, and your company's assets. This comprehensive program is built from the ground up to move beyond basic controls and instill a deep understanding of the principles behind safe operation.

Through a curriculum backed by hundreds of real-world, scenario-based questions, you will dive deep into the five core domains of forklift safety:

  • Operational Safety: Master the stability triangle, rules for ramps and inclines, pedestrian interaction, and turning/maneuvering in any environment.

  • Load Handling: Learn to calculate and respect rated vs. actual capacity, manage off-center and unusual loads, and properly use attachments.

  • Equipment Inspection: Gain the skills to perform a thorough pre-operation inspection, identifying critical defects in hydraulics, steering, brakes, and fuel systems before they lead to failure.

  • Hazard Recognition: Develop a keen eye for workplace hazards, from slippery floors and loading dock edges to hazardous atmospheres and blind spots.

  • Maintenance & Modern Tech: Understand critical safety procedures for battery charging (Lead-Acid and Lithium-Ion), LP tank handling, and the impact of emerging technologies like telematics and proximity sensors.

This course isn't just about passing a test; it’s about developing the professional mindset needed to prevent accidents before they happen. Enroll today to build a foundation of safety, compliance, and confidence that will serve you throughout your career.
