Who is this course for?

  • Aspiring Scaffolding Competent Persons seeking first-time certification.

  • Current Competent Persons and Site Supervisors who want to deepen their expertise.

  • Safety Professionals (CSHOs, CHSTs) responsible for overseeing scaffold operations.

  • Project Managers and Engineers who need to understand the critical requirements of scaffold safety and design.

What you will master through our rigorous, question-based learning model:

  • Advanced Load Calculations: Move beyond the basics to calculate dynamic wind loads, complex counterweight requirements for multi-point suspended scaffolds, and live/dead load ratios in challenging environments.

  • Expert Inspection Protocols: Learn to identify subtle but critical defects, from galvanic corrosion on coastal sites and hairline cracks found with drones, to the failure modes of emerging smart-sensor and composite material systems.

  • Complex Fall Protection & Rescue: Master the application of OSHA standards in non-standard situations, including hybrid systems (PFAS + guardrails), rescue kinematics in confined spaces, and fall protection requirements over dangerous equipment.

  • Deep Dive into OSHA 1926 Subpart L: Understand the intent behind the regulations. Each of our 350+ questions comes with a detailed explanation, referencing the specific OSHA standard, so you know not just the what, but the why.

  • Hazard Mitigation for All Scaffold Types: From supported and mobile scaffolds to complex suspended systems, mast-climbers, and custom-engineered solutions, you will learn to anticipate and mitigate catastrophic failure points.

This course is an active learning tool. By engaging with hundreds of realistic problems and detailed, standards-based feedback, you will build the critical judgment and quick decision-making skills necessary for this vital role.
