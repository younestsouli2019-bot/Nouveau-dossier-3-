Are you struggling to navigate the dense, complex world of the ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code? Are you preparing for a critical career certification like the National Board (NBBI), API 510/570, or Certified Individual (CI), and feeling overwhelmed by the sheer volume of information? This course is your definitive roadmap to success.

"ASME BPV Code Mastery & Exam Prep" is an exhaustive training program designed to transform you from a code user into a code expert. We dive deep into the most critical and frequently tested areas of the ASME BPV Code, focusing on the sections that matter most for certification and real-world application: Section I (Power Boilers), Section IV (Heating Boilers), and the cornerstone of pressure equipment, Section VIII, Divisions 1, 2, & 3 (Pressure Vessels).

This is not just a high-level overview. You will gain a granular understanding of core principles, including:

  • Code Structure & Stamping: Learn to confidently identify the purpose and application of every major ASME and National Board stamp (U, U2, U3, S, H, R, V, VR) and navigate the structure of the codes.

  • Design & Fabrication Mastery: Move beyond theory to apply the rules for material selection (P-Numbers, MTRs), weld joint design (Categories A, B, C, D), nozzle reinforcement (UG-37), and the critical requirements for post-weld heat treatment (PWHT) and impact testing exemptions (UCS-66).

  • Inspection & Testing Protocols: Master the requirements for hydrostatic (UG-99) and pneumatic (UG-100) testing. Differentiate the application, techniques, and acceptance criteria for NDE methods including Radiography (RT), Ultrasonics (UT), Magnetic Particle (MT), and Liquid Penetrant (PT) as defined in Section V.

  • Quality & Compliance Assurance: Understand the essential elements of an ASME Quality Control Manual, the roles and responsibilities of the Authorized Inspector (AI) and Certified Individual (CI), and the crucial new competence requirements detailed in Appendix 47 of the 2025 Code edition.

Built around a comprehensive bank of 300 exam-style multiple-choice questions, each with detailed, code-referenced explanations, this course simulates the certification testing environment. You won’t just memorize answers; you will learn the underlying principles, ensuring you can solve complex problems on the job and in the exam room.

Enroll today to build the confidence and expertise needed to pass your certification exam and become a recognized leader in the field of pressure equipment integrity.
