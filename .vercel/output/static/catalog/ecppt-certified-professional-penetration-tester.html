This comprehensive course is your ultimate guide to mastering the skills required to conquer the eLearnSecurity Certified Professional Penetration Tester (eCPPT) exam and excel in a real-world penetration testing career. Designed to be 100% practical, this program moves beyond theory and dives deep into the hands-on techniques that define modern offensive security.

Throughout this course, you will learn to think and act like a professional penetration tester. We will systematically cover the entire testing process, starting with in-depth information gathering and reconnaissance to build a complete picture of your target. You will then learn to gain initial access by exploiting common web application vulnerabilities like SQL injection and XSS, as well as by performing sophisticated password spraying and brute-force attacks.

The curriculum places a heavy emphasis on the skills that matter most in corporate environments. You will dedicate significant time to mastering Active Directory penetration testing, learning to perform Kerberos attacks, and moving laterally across a compromised network. A crucial part of the course is dedicated to exploit development, where you will learn to manually identify and exploit stack-based buffer overflows on both Windows and Linux systems—a key requirement for the eCPPT.[1][2]

What truly sets this course apart is its focus on the complete engagement lifecycle. You won't just learn how to "get root." You will master the art of pivoting and double pivoting to navigate complex, segmented networks, and learn post-exploitation techniques to maintain persistence. Critically, you will learn how to document your findings and write a commercial-grade, professional penetration testing report—a skill that is directly evaluated in the eCPPT exam and is essential for a successful career. This course provides the hands-on experience and methodological approach needed to pass the challenging eCPPT exam and prove your capabilities as a professional penetration tester
