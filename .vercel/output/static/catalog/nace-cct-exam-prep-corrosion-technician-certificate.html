Are you ready to pass the NACE Certified Corrosion Technician (CCT) exam and advance your career in corrosion control? This comprehensive prep test course is designed to equip you with the knowledge and skills you need to succeed. Aligned with the latest NACE CCT Body of Knowledge, this course provides a thorough review of all key exam domains, ensuring you're fully prepared for every question.

We cover corrosion fundamentals in depth, including electrochemical principles, diverse corrosion types (uniform, pitting, galvanic, etc.), and the influence of environmental factors. You'll gain practical expertise in protective coatings, mastering surface preparation standards, application techniques, crucial inspection methods (DFT, adhesion testing), and effective coating failure analysis. Understand cathodic protection principles, system design considerations, essential testing criteria, and ongoing monitoring practices.

The course also delves into various inspection and testing methods, such as Non-Destructive Examination (NDE) techniques (ultrasound, magnetic particle), thorough visual inspection protocols, accurate documentation practices, and adherence to relevant NACE/AMPP and SSPC standards. Finally, we address critical safety and environmental practices, including comprehensive hazard identification, proper use of PPE, safe confined space entry procedures, and compliance with environmental regulations.

  • Real-world scenarios to enhance your practical application of knowledge

  • Proven tips and strategies to maximize your exam performance and ensure success
