Are you ready to move beyond managing single projects and start leading complex, strategic, transformational change? The Managing Successful Programmes (MSP®) 5th Edition framework is the globally recognized best practice for programme management, and this course is your complete guide to mastering it and achieving your Foundation certification.

Designed for current and aspiring programme managers, project managers, and business change leaders, this course provides a comprehensive deep dive into the MSP framework. We will demystify the core components, ensuring you not only memorize the theory but truly understand how to apply it.

What you will master in this course:

  • The 7 MSP® Principles: Go beyond rote learning to understand the fundamental mindset—the ‘why’—behind successful programmes. We’ll explore how to Lead with purpose, Collaborate across boundaries, and Deal with ambiguity in real-world scenarios.

  • The 7 Governance Themes: Learn how to set up and manage the programme’s control environment. We will cover the ‘what’ of programme management, including Organization (defining roles like the SRO and BCM), Justification (building and maintaining the Business Case), and Benefits Management (ensuring the programme delivers actual value).

  • The 6 Transformational Flow Processes: Follow the complete programme lifecycle—the ‘how’—from the initial idea to the final closure. You'll learn the purpose of each process, from Identify the programme and Design the outcomes through to Embed the outcomes and Close the programme.

Why this course is different:

This course is built around active learning. To prepare you for the rigors of the official exam, you will get access to a question bank of 300 MSP® Foundation (5th Edition) style multiple-choice questions, each with a detailed explanation for the correct and incorrect answers. These scenario-based questions will challenge you to apply your knowledge, targeting the common areas where candidates struggle.

By the end of this course, you will not only have the knowledge required to pass your MSP® Foundation exam with confidence, but you will also have the understanding to be a more effective leader of change in your organization.

Enroll today and take the definitive next step in your strategic management career.
