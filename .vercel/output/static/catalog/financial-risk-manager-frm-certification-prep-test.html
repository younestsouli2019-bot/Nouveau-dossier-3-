Prepare to conquer the Financial Risk Manager (FRM) exam and advance your career in risk management! This comprehensive FRM Exam Prep course is designed to provide you with the knowledge, skills, and confidence you need to succeed on the FRM exam, Part I and Part II. Updated for the 2025 curriculum, this course delves into the core concepts and practical applications of financial risk management, ensuring you are fully equipped to tackle even the most challenging exam questions.

What you'll master in this course:

  • Market Risk: Understand and apply Value at Risk (VaR), Expected Shortfall (ES), volatility modeling (GARCH), and hedging strategies using derivatives.

  • Credit Risk: Learn to measure and manage credit risk, including probability of default (PD), loss given default (LGD), credit default swaps (CDS), and credit risk mitigation techniques.

  • Operational Risk: Explore the key elements of operational risk management, including risk identification, assessment, and mitigation strategies, as well as the Basel III framework.

  • Model Risk: Understand the nuances of model risk, including validation techniques, governance structures, and best practices for managing model limitations.

This course includes:

  • 250+ Hard-Level Practice Questions: Test your knowledge and build exam confidence with a vast library of challenging practice questions, mirroring the difficulty and style of the actual FRM exam.

  • Detailed Explanations: Understand the rationale behind each answer with in-depth explanations, reinforcing your understanding of key concepts.

  • Comprehensive Coverage: Master the entire FRM curriculum, including market risk, credit risk, operational risk, investment risk management, and current issues in financial markets.

  • Updated for 2025: Ensure you're studying the most current material with content aligned to the latest FRM exam syllabus.

Who should take this course:

  • Aspiring Financial Risk Managers preparing for the FRM exam.

  • Risk management professionals seeking to enhance their knowledge and skills.

  • Finance professionals looking to advance their careers in the risk management field.
