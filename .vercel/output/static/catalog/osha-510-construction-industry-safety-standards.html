OSHA 510 (Construction Industry Safety Standards)

Are you responsible for the safety of workers on a construction site? Do you need to confidently navigate the complex world of OSHA regulations to prevent incidents, protect your team, and ensure compliance? This course provides a deep dive into the OSHA 510 Occupational Safety and Health Standards for the Construction Industry, focusing entirely on the 29 CFR 1926 regulations.

Unlike basic awareness courses, this program is designed to equip you with the practical, applicable knowledge needed to lead safety efforts effectively. We move beyond simply reciting the rules and focus on real-world scenarios you face every day. You won't just learn what the rule is; you'll learn how to apply it on your site to identify hazards, select appropriate controls, and build a world-class safety culture.

Throughout this course, you will master the four critical domains of construction safety:

  • OSHA Framework & Compliance: Confidently manage an OSHA inspection, understand citation procedures, and master injury and illness recordkeeping (OSHA 300 Logs).

  • Core Hazard Identification (The Fatal Four & More): Gain expert-level knowledge of the standards for Fall Protection (Subpart M), Electrical Safety (Subpart K), Excavations & Trenching (Subpart P), and Scaffolding (Subpart L).

  • Effective Hazard Control: Learn to apply the hierarchy of controls, from elimination to PPE. Implement robust procedures for Lockout/Tagout (LOTO), Confined Space Entry (Subpart AA), and machine guarding.

  • Safety Program Management: Understand the critical duties of a Competent Person versus a Qualified Person, design effective safety training programs, and develop Emergency Action Plans that work.

This course is essential for Construction Site Supervisors, Foremen, Project Managers, Safety Directors, and any individual with safety responsibilities. It is also an invaluable resource for those preparing for safety certifications such as the CHST or seeking to fulfill the prerequisite for the OSHA 500 Trainer Course.

Enroll today to gain the critical knowledge you need to advance your career, protect your workforce, and become a true safety leader in the construction industry.
