This course provides comprehensive practice exams designed to help you confidently pass the Razorpay Certified Payments Expert Exam. These practice exams are meticulously crafted to mirror the difficulty, content, and structure of the actual certification exam. They cover all the essential domains outlined in the Razorpay certification blueprint, ensuring you are thoroughly prepared.

Key areas covered in these practice exams include:

  • Payment Gateway Integration: Test your knowledge of checkout flows, UPI/NetBanking/credit card setups, custom payment pages, and international currency handling.

  • API & Webhook Mastery: Master idempotency keys, order creation, refunds, subscription APIs, and webhook security (signature verification).

  • Security & Compliance: Assess your understanding of PCI-DSS, RBI tokenization mandates, OTP authentication (EMI), data encryption, and fraud prevention.

  • Dispute & Refund Management: Evaluate your skills in chargeback handling, NACH failure resolution, RBI timelines, and evidence submission.

  • Analytics & Reporting: Sharpen your ability to interpret Razorpay Dashboard metrics (success rates, failure codes), GST invoices, and reconciliation files.

  • Regulatory Frameworks: Gauge your knowledge of FEMA guidelines, GST compliance, KYC norms, and cross-border transaction rules.

  • Advanced Features: Familiarize yourself with Smart Collect, Route, Magic Checkout, and RazorpayX payroll integrations.

These practice exams feature:

  • Scenario-Based Questions: Tackle real-world payment challenges that require critical thinking and problem-solving skills.

  • Challenging Difficulty: Experience the rigor of the actual certification exam.

  • Detailed Explanations: Understand the rationale behind each correct and incorrect answer.

  • Domain-Specific Focus: Reinforce your understanding of each key domain.

  • Up-to-Date Content: Aligned with the latest Razorpay features, RBI guidelines, and PCI standards.

By taking these practice exams, you will:

  • Identify your strengths and weaknesses: Pinpoint areas where you excel and areas where you need to improve.

  • Improve your exam-taking skills: Learn how to manage your time effectively and avoid common mistakes.

  • Boost your confidence: Approach the certification exam with a sense of preparedness and assurance.

  • Increase your chances of passing the Razorpay Certified Payments Expert Exam: Achieve your certification goals and advance your career.

Prepare effectively, practice strategically, and pass with confidence!
