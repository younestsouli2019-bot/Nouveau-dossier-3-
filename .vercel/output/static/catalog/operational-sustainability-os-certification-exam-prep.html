Are you ready to become a certified expert in Operational Sustainability and drive real change within your organization? This comprehensive prep course is designed to equip you with the knowledge and skills you need to confidently pass the Operational Sustainability (OS) Certification exam in 2025 and lead the way in building a more sustainable future.

This course goes beyond simply memorizing definitions. We delve into the critical concepts and practical applications of operational sustainability, covering key domains such as:

  • Carbon Accounting and Scope 3 Emissions: Learn how to accurately measure, manage, and reduce your organization's carbon footprint, including complex Scope 3 emissions across the value chain.

  • Circular Economy Principles: Discover how to implement circular economy models to minimize waste, maximize resource utilization, and design products for longevity and recyclability.

  • Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) Factors: Understand how to integrate ESG considerations into investment decisions, supply chain management, and corporate strategy to drive long-term value creation.

  • Life Cycle Assessment (LCA): Master the methodology of LCA to assess the environmental impacts of products and services throughout their entire lifecycle, from raw material extraction to end-of-life disposal.

  • Sustainable Supply Chain Management: Learn how to build resilient and ethical supply chains that minimize environmental and social risks while ensuring business continuity.

  • Water Stewardship and Resource Efficiency: Develop strategies to reduce water consumption, improve resource efficiency, and minimize environmental impacts in water-stressed regions.

  • Climate Risk Management: Identify, assess, and manage the physical and transitional risks associated with climate change, and develop strategies to build organizational resilience.

Through engaging video lectures, real-world case studies, practice quizzes, and a comprehensive mock exam, you'll gain a deep understanding of the OS Certification body of knowledge and develop the practical skills you need to succeed in the exam and in your career.
