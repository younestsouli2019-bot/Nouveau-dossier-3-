Are you an International Medical Graduate (IMG) aiming to conquer the USMLE and secure a U.S. residency position? The path is challenging, the exams are rigorous, and your performance on them is critical. To succeed, you don't just need to know the material—you need to master the art of answering board-style questions under pressure. This course is your ultimate training ground.

The "ECFMG Certification Prep Test" is a comprehensive, high-yield question bank meticulously designed to simulate the real exam experience and build your clinical reasoning skills. We have crafted hundreds of challenging, application-based multiple-choice questions that cover the full spectrum of topics required for ECFMG certification, from basic sciences to advanced clinical management.

This is more than just a collection of questions; it's a complete learning system. Each question comes with a detailed, in-depth explanation that doesn't just tell you the right answer, but explains the underlying pathophysiology, pharmacology, and clinical logic. We break down why the correct option is best and, just as importantly, why the incorrect options are wrong, helping you solidify your knowledge and avoid common traps.

What you will master in this course:

  • USMLE Step 1 Foundations: Tackle a vast range of questions covering everything from biochemistry and genetics to pathology and pharmacology, all integrated with clinical vignettes.

  • USMLE Step 2 CK Clinical Reasoning: Sharpen your diagnostic and management skills with complex case-based questions in internal medicine, surgery, pediatrics, OB/GYN, and psychiatry.

  • OET & Communication Skills: Practice questions designed to test your understanding of clinical communication, ethics, and patient-centered care—skills vital for the OET and for your future as a U.S. physician.

  • The ECFMG Process: Reinforce your knowledge of the certification process itself, from exam eligibility and application logistics to understanding the Pathways and the NRMP Match.
