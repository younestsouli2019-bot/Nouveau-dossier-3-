The PSAT/NMSQT has gone fully digital, and the old ways of studying are no longer enough. This comprehensive course is your ultimate guide to conquering the new adaptive format and achieving a top-tier score. Designed meticulously for the 2025 administration and beyond, this program moves beyond simple content review to teach you the specific strategies needed to excel on a test that changes based on your performance.

We will deconstruct every aspect of the digital exam. You will learn to navigate the two-stage adaptive system, where your performance on the first module of Reading & Writing and Math determines the difficulty of your second module. Our extensive question bank, featuring 300+ practice questions, is built to mirror this logic, with questions tagged as "Standard," "Adaptive-Low," and "Adaptive-High" to provide a true-to-life practice experience.

This course dives deep into the four core domains tested:

  • Reading & Writing: Move beyond basic comprehension to master Command of Evidence, Words in Context, and the new Rhetorical Synthesis questions. We will analyze complex literary and informational texts, refine your understanding of advanced grammar and punctuation, and teach you how to craft concise, powerful sentences under pressure.

  • Math: Build a rock-solid foundation in all tested areas, from Algebra and Data Analysis to Advanced Math and Geometry/Trigonometry. Crucially, you will learn how to leverage the built-in Desmos graphing calculator, a powerful tool available on every math question, to solve problems faster and more accurately.

For those aiming for the highest scores and National Merit Scholarship recognition, this course includes a dedicated focus on "National Merit Critical" questions—the 99th-percentile difficulty problems that separate a good score from a great one. We will cover advanced topics like vector geometry, complex number theory, and multi-step data interpretation to give you the competitive edge.

Enroll today to transform your test-taking anxiety into the confidence and skills needed to unlock your full potential on the digital PSAT/NMSQT.
