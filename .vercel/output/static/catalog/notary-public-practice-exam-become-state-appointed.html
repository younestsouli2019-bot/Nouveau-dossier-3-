Designed by notary experts, this course provides realistic, challenging practice questions covering ALL essential exam domains, including:

  • Notarial Acts & Procedures: Master acknowledgments, jurats, oaths, affirmations, copy certifications, and protests. Know when and how to apply each act correctly.

  • Identification & Document Handling: Learn to confidently verify IDs (including acceptable and unacceptable forms), handle incomplete documents, and spot expired credentials.

  • Journal Recordkeeping: Understand mandatory journal entries, retention requirements, privacy regulations, and electronic journal best practices.

  • Ethical & Legal Obligations: Navigate conflicts of interest, maintain impartiality, avoid the unauthorized practice of law, and understand fee regulations.

  • Fraud Prevention & Red Flags: Develop skills to detect forgery, undue influence, falsified documents, and assess signer mental capacity.

  • State-Specific Laws (General Principles): Grasp common notary laws applicable across states, covering term lengths, bond/insurance, and remote online notarization (RON) concepts.

  • Electronic Notarization (RON): Familiarize yourself with digital certificates, audio-visual recordings, tamper-evident technology, and RON platform compliance.

    This course features:

  • Realistic Exam Simulation: Practice with multiple-choice questions that mirror the actual exam in difficulty and format.

  • Detailed Explanations: Understand why an answer is correct (or incorrect) with thorough explanations and references.

  • Domain-Specific Practice: Focus your study on areas where you need the most improvement.

  • Up-to-Date Content: Reflecting the latest best practices and general principles.

  • Confidence Building: Prepare to ace your exam and embark on your journey as a trusted Notary Public!
