Through 100+ meticulously crafted, high-difficulty practice questions, you'll master critical BME content domains, including:

  • Medical Device Design & Regulation: FDA 510(k)/PMA pathways, ISO 13485, risk management (ISO 14971), biocompatibility (ISO 10993), EU MDR

  • Clinical Equipment & Instrumentation: Ventilators, dialysis machines, pacemakers, safety standards (IEC 60601), VADs

  • Ethics & Professional Practice: Informed consent, human trials (IRB), data privacy (HIPAA), intellectual property, Declaration of Helsinki, Common Rule

  • Emerging Technologies: Wearable sensors, neuroprosthetics, CRISPR-based therapies, nanomedicine, cybersecurity
