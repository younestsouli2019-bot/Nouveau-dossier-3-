Are you ready to not just pass the Fall Protection Competent Person exam, but to master the critical judgment skills that save lives on the job site?

Built from a database of over 250 hard-level, expert-vetted questions, this program is engineered to challenge your knowledge of OSHA 29 CFR 1926 Subpart M and the latest ANSI Z359 standards.

Who is this course for?

  • Aspiring or current Site Safety Professionals

  • Construction Superintendents and Foremen

  • Project Managers seeking to understand site liability

  • Anyone preparing for a corporate or third-party Competent Person certification exam.

What makes this course different?
We don't just teach you the rules; we teach you how to think like a seasoned safety professional. Our questions are not simple "what is the height of a guardrail?" queries. They are complex, multi-step scenarios pulled from real-world OSHA violations and incident reports, focusing on:

  • Complex Calculations: Master total fall distance, swing fall, and anchorage strength calculations under dynamic loads.

  • Hazard Recognition: Learn to spot subtle but critical equipment defects, non-compliant installations, and the fatal flaws in a rescue plan.

  • Regulatory Nuance: Confidently navigate the gray areas of Subpart M, from multi-employer worksite responsibilities to the proper use of a Fall Protection Plan.

  • Judgment & Authority: Understand the full scope of your duties, from training and inspection to exercising your stop-work authority when it matters most.

Every single question comes with a detailed, per-option explanation, clarifying not just why the correct answer is right, but precisely how the wrong answers violate specific OSHA standards.

By the end of this course, you will have the confidence and validated knowledge to pass your exam, implement a bulletproof fall protection program, and become the go-to safety authority on your worksite.
