Practical Network Penetration Tester (PNPT)

BackBundles

Are you ready to move beyond basic security concepts and prove you have the practical, hands-on skills required to be a professional penetration tester? This course is your comprehensive guide to mastering the art of the real-world network penetration test and preparing you to conquer the highly-respected Practical Network Penetration Tester (PNPT) certification exam.

This is not a theoretical course. We dive deep into the practical methodology that security professionals use every day. We will take you through the entire lifecycle of a simulated engagement, from initial reconnaissance to final reporting, focusing on the techniques and mindset required to succeed in the field and in the exam.

What you will master:

  • Reconnaissance & OSINT: You'll learn how to think like an attacker, gathering intelligence from public sources (OSINT), performing active network scanning with Nmap, and enumerating services to build a complete map of your target's attack surface. We’ll cover DNS, cloud asset discovery, and finding initial clues to guide your attack.

  • Vulnerability Analysis & Exploitation: Go beyond just running scanners. You will learn to identify, analyze, and exploit vulnerabilities in a variety of systems. We will place a heavy emphasis on the number one target in corporate environments: Active Directory. You will master techniques like LLMNR poisoning with Responder, NTLM relaying with Impacket, Kerberoasting, and abusing AD misconfigurations to move from zero to Domain Admin.

  • Post-Exploitation & Lateral Movement: Gaining initial access is just the beginning. This course will teach you what to do next. You will learn critical post-exploitation techniques for both Windows and Linux, including privilege escalation, establishing persistence, and pivoting. You'll master how to use compromised machines to access deeper, more secure parts of the network, creating tunnels and moving laterally until you reach your objective.

  • Professional Reporting: A penetration test is only as good as its report. You will learn how to document your findings, write clear reproduction steps, articulate business impact to executives, and provide actionable remediation guidance that helps organizations truly improve their security posture.

If you are serious about a career in offensive security and want a certification that proves you can do the job, not just answer questions about it, this is the course for you. Enroll now and take the next step toward becoming a Practical Network Penetration Tester.
