Are you ready to build a successful, family-sustaining career in the skilled trades? This course is your definitive guide to mastering the North America's Building Trades Unions (NABTU) Multi-Craft Core Curriculum (MC3) and acing your certification exam. Designed for aspiring apprentices, this program provides the essential knowledge you need to start your journey in the union construction industry.

We have meticulously crafted this curriculum to align perfectly with the NABTU MC3 framework, focusing on the four critical domains that form the foundation of a modern trades career. Through a comprehensive library of over 300 practice questions, detailed explanations, and realistic scenarios, you will gain the confidence and competence to excel.

What you will master:

  • Safety & OSHA (30% of Exam): Go beyond basic compliance. We dive deep into OSHA's most critical construction standards, including fall protection at 6+ feet, Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedures for multi-trade sites, confined space entry protocols, and silica dust control. You'll learn not just the rules, but the why behind them, preparing you for a culture of safety.

  • Union Principles (20% of Exam): Understand the power of solidarity. This module demystifies the structure of building trades unions, covering collective bargaining, grievance procedures, the history of the labor movement, and the benefits of a union apprenticeship. You'll learn what it means to be a union member and the rights and responsibilities that come with it.

  • Green Construction (25% of Exam): Build the future. The construction industry is evolving, and green building is at its core. We cover key sustainability concepts, from LEED credit categories and low-VOC materials to energy-efficient systems and EPA stormwater management. You'll be prepared for the carbon-neutral, sustainable jobsites of today and tomorrow.

  • Trade Fundamentals (25% of Exam): Learn the language of the jobsite. This section builds your practical knowledge, teaching you how to read blueprint symbols, perform essential construction math for measurements and estimates, and identify the tools and techniques used across various core trades like electrical, plumbing, and carpentry.

This course is more than just test prep; it’s a career launchpad. By the end of this program, you will not only be prepared to pass the NABTU MC3 certification exam but will also possess the foundational knowledge that will make you a valuable and sought-after candidate for any registered apprenticeship program.

Enroll today and take the first step toward a rewarding, lifelong career in the union skilled trades!
