This comprehensive Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) practice test is designed to help intermediate-level professionals prepare for CTI certification exams. The test consists of 100 multiple-choice questions divided into two parts, each focusing on key concepts and practical knowledge required for a CTI analyst.


Key topics covered include:

  • The CIA Triad (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability)

  • Types of hackers and their motivations

  • Attack vectors and techniques

  • The intelligence cycle and its components

  • Threat analysis and reporting

  • Incident response and recovery

  • Advanced Persistent Threats (APTs) and nation-state actors

  • Social engineering attacks and defenses

  • Technical and physical security measures

  • Legal and ethical considerations in CTI
