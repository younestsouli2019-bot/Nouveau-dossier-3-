ASME Quality Program for Suppliers (QPS)

BackBundles

In today's complex industrial landscape, a robust and verifiable supply chain is not just an advantage—it's a necessity. The ASME Quality Program for Suppliers (QPS) provides the definitive framework for ensuring your suppliers meet the highest standards of quality and reliability. This comprehensive course is your single source for mastering the ASME QPS:2021 standard, designed to transform you into an expert in modern supplier quality assurance.

Whether you are preparing for a certification audit, aiming to improve your organization's supply chain performance, or seeking to advance your career in quality management, this course provides the in-depth knowledge and practical skills you need. We move beyond theory to give you real-world, actionable insights grounded in the latest industry trends.

Throughout this course, you will dive deep into the four critical domains of the ASME QPS:

  • The QPS Framework: Understand the structure and scope of ASME QPS:2021, from raw materials to services. We will dissect the key clauses, explore the different certification classes (Small, Medium, Large), and clarify the critical relationship between QPS and the ISO 9001 standard. You'll master the function of Technical Agreements to ensure requirements are perfectly aligned.

  • Risk-Based Supplier Management: Learn to implement a modern, risk-based approach to the entire supplier lifecycle. You will master techniques for supplier selection, create scorecards using key performance metrics (OTD, PPM), and develop robust Quality Agreements. We will place special emphasis on emerging requirements, including integrating Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) clauses into your sourcing strategy.

  • Audit & Compliance Mastery: Gain the confidence to lead or excel in formal audits. This course provides a detailed breakdown of Stage I (document review) and Stage II (implementation) audit protocols. You will learn the precise criteria for grading nonconformances (Critical, Major, Minor) and manage the Corrective Action (CAPA) process to ensure effective, permanent solutions.

  • Integration & Continuous Improvement: Discover how to make your QMS a dynamic, value-adding part of your business. We will cover the harmonization of QPS with technical standards (e.g., ASME B31.3, Section IX), the integration of digital QMS tools, and leveraging technologies like AI for risk scoring and blockchain for material traceability to drive continuous improvement and operational excellence.

This course is packed with scenario-based examples and practical insights to prepare you for any challenge. By the end, you will not only understand the requirements of the ASME QPS standard but will be fully equipped to implement, manage, and audit a world-class supplier quality program.

Enroll today and take the definitive step toward becoming a leader in industrial supplier quality.
