IMD Complex Problem Solving (FrED Framework)

BackBundles

Are you facing increasingly complex, ambiguous problems where the old solutions no longer work? In a world defined by volatility, uncertainty, complexity, and ambiguity (VUCA), making high-stakes decisions based on "gut feel" alone is a recipe for failure. This course equips you with a powerful, repeatable, and battle-tested methodology for navigating your toughest professional challenges: the Complex Problem Solving (FrED) framework, developed at the world-renowned IMD Business School.

This isn't just theory; it's a practical toolkit for action. We will guide you through the three core stages of the FrED framework, transforming the way you think, analyze, and act.

  • FRAME: Learn to deconstruct problems, identify and mitigate cognitive biases, map stakeholder interests, and, most importantly, ensure you are solving the right problem from the outset. Avoid the trap of finding the perfect solution to the wrong question.

  • EXPLORE: Move beyond surface-level symptoms to uncover true root causes. You will learn to apply structured analytical tools like the 5 Whys and Fishbone diagrams, use data triangulation to validate insights, and generate a robust set of potential solutions.

  • DECIDE: Make your final choice with clarity and confidence. We will teach you how to evaluate strategic trade-offs, use risk-weighted scoring models, and build clear, actionable implementation roadmaps that ensure your decision becomes a reality.

Throughout this course, you will engage with real-world scenarios drawn from 2025 case studies in technology, healthcare, energy, and finance. You will tackle challenges like mitigating AI bias in analysis, navigating geopolitical volatility in supply chains, and making difficult ESG trade-offs.

This course is for any professional—from senior leaders and consultants to project and product managers—who wants to upgrade their decision-making process. If you want to stop reacting and start architecting robust, well-reasoned solutions, this course will provide you with the structure and confidence to do so. Enroll today and learn the framework that top executives use to win.
