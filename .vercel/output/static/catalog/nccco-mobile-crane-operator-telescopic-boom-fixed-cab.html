Ready to launch a high-paying career as a certified crane operator or finally ace that tough NCCCO exam? This is the only course you will need.

The NCCCO Telescopic Boom - Fixed Cab (TSS) exam is the gold standard for the industry, but it's also a significant challenge, testing a deep knowledge of safety, regulations, and complex operations. This course is your complete, step-by-step roadmap to success, designed to turn the exam blueprint into practical, retainable knowledge.

We have meticulously structured this program around the four core domains of the NCCCO exam, leaving no stone unturned:

  • Operations (40% of Exam): Go beyond theory with real-world scenarios. Master crane setup on various terrains, learn to handle loads near maximum capacity, understand the dynamics of boom positioning, and execute safe pick-and-carry operations.

  • Safety & Compliance (25% of Exam): Navigate the complex regulations of OSHA 1926 Subpart CC and ASME B30.5 with confidence. We demystify power line clearance tables, hazard identification, and critical shutdown protocols so they become second nature.

  • Technical Knowledge (20% of Exam): Get hands-on with the machine—virtually. Learn every critical component, from wire rope inspection criteria and replacement standards to hydraulic systems and, most importantly, the art and science of reading complex load charts.

  • Site Protocols (15% of Exam): A safe operator is part of a safe team. We cover essential communication protocols, rigging fundamentals, lift planning for critical picks, and emergency procedures for scenarios like power loss or hydraulic failure.

What truly sets this course apart is our exclusive exam-simulation question bank. You will get access to over 300 NCCCO-style practice questions, each with detailed explanations for the correct and incorrect answers. This active learning method targets common operator misconceptions and ensures you understand the "why" behind every rule.

This course is built for everyone. If you're brand new to the industry, we will build your knowledge from the ground up. If you're an experienced rigger or oiler looking to move into the operator's seat, we will fill in the gaps. If you're a safety manager, this course will give you the insight you need to lead safe lifting operations.

Stop worrying about the unknown and start building your future. Enroll now and take the most important step towards earning your NCCCO certification.
