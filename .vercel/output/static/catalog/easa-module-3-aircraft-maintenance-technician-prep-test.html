Are you preparing for the EASA Module 3 Aircraft Maintenance Technician exam and finding the electrical fundamentals challenging? This course will equip you with the knowledge and practical skills required to confidently understand, apply and troubleshoot aircraft electrical systems, and pass the EASA Module 3 exam. EASA Module 3 often presents a challenge to new technicians and this course is designed to help you better understand and retain the knowledge you need to pass, and for your career as a technician.

This comprehensive course thoroughly covers all areas of the EASA Module 3 Electrical Fundamentals syllabus. You will gain a solid understanding of the principles behind DC and AC circuits, as well as key electrical components. You will explore areas such as: Ohms Law, Kirchhoff's Laws, electromagnetism, capacitance, inductance, transformers, DC motors and AC generators. Each topic will be delivered through detailed video lectures. The course will also cover different electrical components used in aircraft systems and focus on practical examples and real-world applications to enhance your understanding of how these concepts are applied in an aircraft maintenance context.

This course provides step by step examples and practical applications to help you learn each concept and how to apply them, plus numerous quizzes to help solidify your understanding. The course also includes a full length 250 question practice exam.

By the end of this course, you will not only be thoroughly prepared to pass the EASA Module 3 Electrical Fundamentals exam, but will have built a solid foundation for a long career as an aircraft technician. This course is ideal for aspiring aircraft maintenance technicians, or anyone wanting a solid understanding of the fundamentals of electrical systems in aircraft
