Conquer the ONCC Oncology Nursing Certification Exam!

Are you ready to achieve your OCN® or CPHON® credential? This high-difficulty practice exam course is meticulously designed to simulate the rigor and complexity of the actual ONCC exam, ensuring you're fully prepared to pass with confidence. It goes beyond simple memorization, challenging your clinical judgment and application of evidence-based oncology nursing principles.

What Makes This Course Unique?

  • Realistic Exam Simulation: Over 200+ challenging multiple-choice questions (MCQs) that mirror the ONCC exam blueprint.

  • High-Difficulty Questions: Focus on prioritization, complex clinical scenarios, and nuanced application of oncology nursing knowledge.

  • Comprehensive Coverage: Questions cover ALL major ONCC content domains, including:

    • Cancer Pathophysiology & Staging (TNM, Biomarkers)

    • Treatment Modalities (Chemotherapy, Immunotherapy, Targeted Therapy, Radiation, CAR T-cell)

    • Symptom Management & Treatment Toxicities (Neutropenia, Mucositis, Neuropathies, Cytokine Release Syndrome, Extravasation)

    • Oncologic Emergencies (Sepsis, Tumor Lysis Syndrome, Spinal Cord Compression, SVC Syndrome)

    • Palliative & Supportive Care (Pain Management, Bowel Regimens, Dyspnea, End-of-Life Care)

    • Psychosocial & Survivorship Care (Distress Screening, Financial Toxicity, Survivorship Care Plans, Late Effects)

    • Safe Handling & Administration (Chemotherapy Certification, PPE, Hazardous Drug Disposal)

    • Pediatric Oncology (ALL, Central Lines, Growth Preservation, Family-Centered Care) - Relevant for CPHON® candidates

  • Detailed Explanations: Each question includes a thorough explanation of the correct answer and why the distractors are incorrect, referencing NCCN guidelines, ONS standards, and ASCO recommendations.

  • Scenario-Based Learning: Develop strong clinical judgment.

  • Patient Safety Focus: Reinforce essential aspects of oncology nursing practice.

Who Is This Course For?

  • Registered Nurses (RNs) preparing for the OCN® (Oncology Certified Nurse) exam.

  • Oncology nurses seeking a challenging review of core concepts.
