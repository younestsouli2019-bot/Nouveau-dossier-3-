This comprehensive prep course is meticulously designed to provide you with the essential knowledge, practical skills, and confidence needed to not only pass the certification exam but also to excel in real-world engineering applications using Abaqus. This isn't just another exam prep; it's your personalized guide to mastering Abaqus and gaining a coveted industry credential.

This course goes beyond basic theory and offers a deep dive into the most challenging aspects of Abaqus. You'll gain a thorough understanding of the core concepts, and practical experience by focusing on key exam areas, such as:

  • Advanced Material Modeling: You'll learn to accurately define and simulate a vast range of material behaviors, encompassing hyperelasticity, plasticity, advanced composites, and more. You will master the art of choosing the correct model for each application using both built in and user-defined material models.

  • Complex Contact Mechanics: You'll become proficient in modelling a variety of contact interactions, including frictional effects, large sliding, and separation. We will extensively explore how to accurately model bolted connections, spot welds, and other complex interfaces.

  • High Speed Explicit Dynamic Analysis: You'll delve deep into the Abaqus/Explicit module, understand the underlying time integration scheme, and learn how to simulate high-speed impact events, crash tests, and highly non-linear dynamic scenarios with confidence.

  • Multi-Physics Thermal Analysis Simulations: You'll gain a thorough understanding of heat transfer in Abaqus, covering conduction, convection, and radiation and how to perform coupled thermal-stress simulations to model thermal expansion, contraction and associated stresses.

  • Robust Mesh Generation: You will master the art of generating optimized meshes, and learn how mesh density can affect accuracy, while also exploring adaptive meshing techniques to efficiently analyze intricate geometries.

  • Abaqus Commands and Keywords: You will understand the function of a large array of key commands and keywords that are used in an Abaqus simulation, from the *STEP command to all the output requests, including material and contact parameters.

Through a blend of engaging video lectures, practical hands-on exercises, challenging practice questions, and extensive example walkthroughs, you'll solidify your understanding of each critical concept. You will not only learn the theory but also see it in action, becoming capable of handling complex problems in Abaqus. We will explore several practical examples, explaining the nuances of different settings and techniques that are used in the industry. You'll learn to troubleshoot common errors, optimize your simulations for accuracy and performance, and gain the skills needed for long-term success.
