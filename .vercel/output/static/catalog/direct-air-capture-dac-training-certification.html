Direct Air Capture (DAC) is poised to become one of the most critical technologies in the fight against climate change, creating a multi-trillion-dollar industry and a massive new field for skilled professionals. But for those looking to enter or lead in this space, the landscape is complex, fragmented, and difficult to navigate. How do you distinguish between different technologies? Which training programs are credible? What skills truly matter?

This course is the definitive guide to that landscape. We cut through the noise to provide a comprehensive, structured overview of the entire DAC ecosystem, from the fundamental science to the market dynamics. Designed for aspiring and current professionals, this masterclass provides the crucial context and knowledge needed to make strategic career and business decisions.

In this course, you will:

  • Master the Core Technologies: Go beyond the headlines to understand the real-world engineering challenges and advantages of different DAC pathways, including solid sorbent (TSA), liquid solvent (calcium looping), and emerging methods.

  • Navigate the Policy & Economic Drivers: Learn to analyze the policies like the 45Q tax credit, the Inflation Reduction Act (IRA), and carbon markets that are shaping the financial viability of DAC projects worldwide.

  • Become an Expert in High-Integrity MRV & LCA: Understand the critical principles of Monitoring, Reporting, and Verification (MRV) and Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) that separate high-quality, permanent carbon removal from greenwashing.

  • Identify Your Career Pathway: Explore the diverse range of high-demand roles in the DAC industry—from engineering and geoscience to policy, finance, and community engagement—and learn what it takes to land them.

  • Evaluate Training & Certifications: Get a comprehensive review of the leading professional training programs and certifications offered by institutions like Climeworks Academy, Oxford Net Zero, and the DOE to choose the path that’s right for you.

This course is packed with real-world examples and case studies, preparing you for practical application and potential future industry certifications. Whether you are an engineer looking to pivot, a policymaker crafting regulations, an investor evaluating startups, or a student charting your future, this course will provide the foundational knowledge you need to succeed.
