Are you ready to launch your career in cybersecurity and become a certified ethical hacker? The eLearnSecurity Junior Penetration Tester (eJPT) is one of the most respected, hands-on certifications for entering the world of offensive security. This course is your comprehensive roadmap to mastering the required skills and confidently passing the exam.

This isn't just another theory-based course where you passively watch videos. We believe in learning by doing. This entire course is built around practical, real-world application, designed to mirror the methodology and toolset used by professional penetration testers every day. You will move beyond theory and gain tangible skills using the exact tools of the trade.

We will guide you step-by-step through the complete penetration testing lifecycle, from initial reconnaissance to final reporting. You will learn how to think like an attacker, identify vulnerabilities, execute exploits, and pivot through a network. To ensure you are fully prepared for the exam format, this course includes a massive, exclusive question bank of 300+ eJPT-style multiple-choice questions, each with detailed explanations to solidify your understanding.

Throughout this course, you will master:

The Complete Pentesting Methodology: Understand and apply the structured phases of an engagement, including reconnaissance, scanning, exploitation, and post-exploitation, based on industry standards like PTES.

Network Scanning & Enumeration: Go deep with Nmap to discover live hosts, identify open ports and running services, fingerprint operating systems, and enumerate critical services like SMB, SNMP, and DNS.

Exploitation Fundamentals: Gain hands-on control of target systems using the Metasploit Framework. You'll learn to find, select, and launch exploits against vulnerable services.

Web Application Hacking: Learn to identify and exploit the most common web vulnerabilities, including SQL Injection (SQLi), Cross-Site Scripting (XSS), and Local/Remote File Inclusion (LFI/RFI) using tools like Burp Suite.

Post-Exploitation Techniques: Learn what to do after the hack. Escalate your privileges on both Windows and Linux, pivot to other network segments, establish persistence, and exfiltrate data.

Reporting: Understand how to document your findings, rate risks using the CVSS framework, and create a professional penetration testing report.

By the end of this course, you won't just understand penetration testing—you'll have the hands-on skills and confidence to pass the eJPT exam and take the first critical step in your ethical hacking career.

Enroll now and start your journey to becoming a certified penetration tester!
