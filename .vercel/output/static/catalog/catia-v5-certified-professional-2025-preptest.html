We delve deep into the core areas tested on the exam, including:

  • Advanced Part Design: Learn to create complex parts with intricate features, using advanced techniques like multi-section solids, sweeps, and lofts. We go beyond the basics to help you master the most complex modeling tools.

  • Sheet Metal Design: Get advanced expertise in sheet metal modeling, including flanges, bends, hems, and louvers while also understanding flat pattern generation with all bend details.
