7-day money-back guarantee

If a course doesn't meet your expectations, contact us within 7 days of purchase with your order reference and we'll issue a full refund to your original payment method.

  3. We confirm and process the refund within 5 business days.

Non-refundable services

Services delivered in full, or where clearly stated at the time of purchase, may be non-refundable. This is reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

Disputes

If you believe a charge is incorrect, provide evidence and context to younesdgc@gmail.com. We respond within 5 business days and work toward a fair resolution before any chargeback is raised.
