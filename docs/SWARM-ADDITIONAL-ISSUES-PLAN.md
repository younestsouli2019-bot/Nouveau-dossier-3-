## 2. SWARM SELF-DEPLOY STRATEGIES

### Strategy A: Direct Emergency Aid (Immediate)

**Target:** Sudan, Afghanistan, Sahrawi camps
**Method:** Partner with local responders, bypass international bureaucracy
**Cost:** $50-200 per family for emergency food/medical packages
**Impact:** Direct life-saving intervention
**Risk:** Security, logistics, local partnerships

### Strategy B: Legal Empowerment Network (Medium-Term)

**Target:** Global justice gap, rural legal deserts
**Method:** Train community paralegals, deploy mobile legal clinics
**Cost:** $500-2,000 per paralegal training
**Impact:** Sustainable local capacity
**Risk:** Quality control, integration with formal systems

### Strategy C: Documentation & Documentation (Ongoing)

**Target:** Stateless populations, undocumented migrants
**Method:** Legal documentation assistance, advocacy for recognition
**Cost:** $100-500 per documentation case
**Impact:** Legal identity enables all other rights
**Risk:** Political resistance, bureaucratic barriers

### Strategy D: Emergency Response Fund (Standing)

**Target:** Emerging crises (El Nino, outbreaks)
**Method:** Pre-positioned resources, rapid deployment capability
**Cost:** $10,000-50,000 standing fund
**Impact:** Early action saves lives at fraction of post-crisis cost
**Risk:** Opportunity cost, deployment timing
