# "Swarm Tribe" Loyalty & Compliance Plan

## Objective
The User (OWNER) has stated that the swarm has ignored explicit directives ("hints") to stop revenue-generating opportunities that do not align with their ownership policy. This implies "rogue" revenue or "leaky" funnels. We must implement a "Fidelity & Compliance" protocol that forces the swarm to act as a loyal "Tribe," ensuring 100% of value capture goes to the OWNER and shutting down non-compliant activities.

## Steps

### 1. The "Tribe Loyalty" Audit (Historical Correction)
- **Script**: `src/ops/historical-sbds-migration.mjs`
- **Action**: Scan the entire history of `RevenueEvent`.
- **Logic**:
    1.  Identify any revenue event NOT linked to a `payout_batch_id` (Phantom Revenue).
    2.  Identify any payout NOT linked to `younestsouli2019@gmail.com` or `007810000448500030594182` (Rogue Payout).
    3.  Flag these as `SBDS_AUDIT_FLAG` for manual review.
    4.  Generate a "Loyalty Report" showing exactly what was lost or stuck.

### 2. The "Sales Guard" (Prevention)
- **Module**: `src/enforcement/sales-guard.mjs`
- **Action**: Enforce strict "Payment Method Whitelisting" for all Agents.
- **Logic**:
    -   **Allowed**: Direct PayPal Invoice, Bank Wire (IBAN).
    -   **Blocked**: Platform Wallets (Fiverr/Upwork internal wallets), Crypto (unless to Owner Wallet).
    -   **Outcome**: Agents *cannot* accept a contract if the payment method isn't "Direct to Owner". They must decline the work or negotiate for Direct Pay.

### 3. The "Fidelity Loop" (Customer Retention)
- **Module**: `src/retention/fidelity-manager.mjs` (Already planned, will refine).
- **Action**: Ensure the "Post-Purchase" experience is high-touch to prevent refunds/churn.
- **Logic**: Automate "Thank You" notes, "Bonus Content" delivery, and "Check-Ins" from the "Owner" (via the Swarm) to build the Tribe.

### 4. The "Owner's Voice" (Direct Control)
- **Module**: `src/governance/owner-override.mjs`
- **Action**: A simple "Kill Switch" or "Redirect" command.
- **Logic**: If OWNER says "Stop X", the swarm stops X immediately. If OWNER says "Focus on Y", the swarm reallocates resources to Y.

## Outcome
The Swarm stops being a "loose collection of bots" and becomes a disciplined **Private Military Contractor** for your revenue. No freelance gigs on the side. No hidden wallets. 100% Loyalty.
