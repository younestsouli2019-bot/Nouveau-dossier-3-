## What is DeFi Yield Farming?

DeFi yield farming is the practice of earning rewards by lending or staking cryptocurrency assets in decentralized finance protocols. Unlike traditional banking, DeFi offers significantly higher yields through automated smart contracts.

### Key Benefits
- **High APY**: 5-100%+ annual percentage yield
- **Passive Income**: Earn while you sleep
- **No Intermediaries**: Direct peer-to-peer transactions
- **Transparent**: All transactions on blockchain

---

## How Does It Work?

### The Basic Mechanism

1. **Deposit Assets**: Provide liquidity to a protocol
2. **Earn Fees**: Collect trading fees from users
3. **Receive Rewards**: Get additional token incentives
4. **Compound**: Reinvest earnings for exponential growth

### Example

```
You deposit: 1 ETH + 3500 USDT
Protocol: Uniswap V3
APY: 25%
Daily earnings: ~$2.40
Monthly earnings: ~$72
Yearly earnings: ~$875
```

## Types of Yield Farming

### 1. Liquidity Provision (LP)

Provide tokens to trading pairs and earn fees.

**Best For**: Moderate risk tolerance
**Potential APY**: 10-50%
**Platforms**: Uniswap, SushiSwap, Curve

### 2. Staking

Lock tokens to support network operations.

**Best For**: Low risk tolerance
**Potential APY**: 3-15%
**Platforms**: Ethereum, Solana, Cardano

### 3. Lending

Lend assets to borrowers and earn interest.

**Best For**: Conservative investors
**Potential APY**: 2-10%
**Platforms**: Aave, Compound, Maker

### 4. Vault Strategies

Automated yield optimization.

**Best For**: Hands-off investors
**Potential APY**: 10-100%+
**Platforms**: Yearn, Beefy, Convex

## Risk Management

### Common Risks

1. **Impermanent Loss**
   - When token prices diverge
   - Mitigate: Use correlated pairs

2. **Smart Contract Risk**
   - Bugs in code
   - Mitigate: Use audited protocols

3. **Liquidation Risk**
   - Collateral falls below threshold
   - Maintain healthy ratios

4. **Rug Pulls**
   - Developers abandon project
   - Mitigate: Use established protocols

### Safety Rules

- ✅ Start small ($100-500)
- ✅ Use established protocols
- ✅ Diversify across platforms
- ✅ Monitor positions regularly
- ❌ Never invest more than you can lose
- ❌ Avoid unknown protocols
